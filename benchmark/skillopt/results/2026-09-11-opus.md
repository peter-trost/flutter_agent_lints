# SkillOpt run, 2026-09-11, target and optimizer claude-opus-5

Config `configs/default.yaml`: 23 train, 12 validation, 3 test tasks; batch
10, edit budget 3, 3 epochs planned. Stopped after step 3 of 9: the
validation gate was at its ceiling, so no further regular edit could be
accepted, and epoch-boundary slow updates are force-accepted without a gate.

| Stage | Validation (12 tasks) | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| baseline | 10 clean | `ticker` wrote the constructor with the class name; `merge_streams` hit `void_checks` |
| step 1 | 11 clean, accepted | 80-column sample, `var` on mutable fields, consuming a future taken from a collection |
| step 2 | 11 clean, rejected | equal to best |
| step 3 | 12 clean, accepted | `sort_constructors_first`, a complete `@immutable` `User` sample, positional booleans |

Held-out test (the 3 agent-benchmark tasks, single-shot): untrained skill
2 of 3 clean, trained skill 1 of 3. Three tasks cannot separate the two;
the agent benchmark is the test that counts.

Every accepted edit was reviewed before shipping. Two samples were not
self-contained (caught by `tool/check_samples.dart`), one sentence about
`discarded_futures` claimed that returning a future from a sync body fires
the rule (it does not), and one clause was an artifact of a task that
demanded a positional boolean the ruleset forbids. The smoke run before
this one found a real defect in the skill: it sent the model to
`package:meta` for `@immutable` inside a Flutter app.

About 100 `claude -p` calls in total, roughly 25 minutes of wall clock.
